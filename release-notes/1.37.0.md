## What is new

- The desktop app for Windows and Linux is rebuilt in Rust. It starts faster, uses much less memory and the download is a lot smaller. Everything looks and works the same, and your settings, computers and paired phones carry over.
- The relay on your computer now runs inside the app itself, also in Rust.
- The relay on your server is fully in Rust and keeps updating itself from Settings → Updates.

## Good to know

- Older versions of the app update to this one by themselves. On Windows the new version installs in the same place, so your shortcuts keep working.
- Dropping a file into a chat now always uploads it into the project, also on this computer.
