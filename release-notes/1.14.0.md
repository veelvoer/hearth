## Made for everyone

- Large text: the phone app was checked at a very large system font. Chat titles in the header can now use two lines, and screens wrap instead of cutting words.
- Screen readers: the send, stop and message buttons now say what they do.
- Dialogs on the phone all share the same background as the rest of the app.
- Computer: plain panels are now the default, like on the phone, and the dashboard tiles and chat title line up better. Frosted glass is still a switch in Settings.
