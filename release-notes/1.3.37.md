## Fixes

- Options: scrolling a row of choices (autonomy, model, effort) sideways no longer leaves the selected highlight behind.
- Week slider on Home: every day's label sits under its own piece of the bar again, and days too close together step down to a second line instead of overlapping or disappearing.
- Sharing a chat with nobody yet no longer hides it from the All list. It moves to Shared once someone joins.

## Icons

- Familiar icons next to one-word buttons (Share, Remove, Add, Test call, Check for updates, and more).
- The chat header uses icons only (share, talk, options) so it never crowds. Shared chats get their own icon for the usage choice instead of a second "Options".
- Shorter Share dialog, and "Stop sharing" only shows when someone has joined.
