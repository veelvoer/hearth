## Settings as menus, a full dashboard on the phone, and the checklist fix

- Settings now open like the settings of other apps: a menu of groups (Computers, Updates, Notifications, Look and feel, Server, Tools, Help) and each opens its own page. On the computer the groups are on the left with a find box, and the cards of the chosen group on the right. There is a new About page at the end.
- Phone: Settings > Look and feel > "Full dashboard on Home" adds everything the computer dashboard shows to the Home screen: totals, models, projects and tools of the last 30 days.
- Support (owner): if your server cannot give the checklist yet, you now see why and still see the open reports, instead of "Loading…" that never ends.
