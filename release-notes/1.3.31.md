## What is new

- **Shared chats are normal chats now.** Share any chat that lives on your server: press Share in the chat (desktop header, phone header), make an invite link and send it. Your friend pastes it under "Join a shared chat" and the chat shows up in their list like any other, with the same screen.
- **Friends can read everything, but what they send needs your OK.** When a friend sends a prompt you get a notification: a card in the chat and a message in the app, a small window on top of everything on the desktop (when Hearth is not in front), and a pop-up notification on the phone. You can change the prompt, choose how much Claude may do, and run it, or deny it. Or give that friend full permission so their prompts run at once.
- **It always runs on your server**, in the same chat, as "Name: prompt", so Claude knows who is talking.
- **The same shared chats on all your devices**: the chats you were invited to are kept on your server, so every phone and computer of yours shows them.
- A friend's key opens only that one chat. Remove a friend or stop sharing at any time and the access ends at once.
- The separate "Shared" tab and the old "shared chat room" are gone: this replaces them.
