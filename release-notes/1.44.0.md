## New

- **The watch face follows your Hearth theme.** Pick a theme in the watch app (your watch, your computer, or a preset) and the ring, the date, the Claude status line and the hour marks on the Hearth face take its colours. You can also set the time to the theme colour.
- **Much more to set on the watch face.** Long-press the face and choose Edit:
  - What the ring shows (Claude 5-hour usage, battery, steps goal, or nothing) and how thick it is.
  - Time style (large, thin, bold, medium) and colour (white, warm white, theme colour, theme text).
  - Date on or off, in three styles. Claude status line on or off. Hour marks. A soft or deep colour background.
  - Four spots for any complication: left and right (Claude week and Claude chats by default), top and bottom (empty by default).
  - Whether the ring stays, dimmed, on the always-on screen.
- **Chats open on a card** that shows what Claude is doing right now ("Reading Api.kt", "Running npm test") and its last steps. When it is done, the card says when, and the answer follows in screen-sized pieces you can read with the bezel.
- **Pinned chats on the watch.** The watch shows your pinned chats first, with the names you gave them, the same as on your phone.

## Smoother

- Lists use the whole round screen: rows are full width in the middle and shrink and fade towards the top and bottom, like the watch's own settings.
- Turning the bezel glides the list instead of jumping, with a light tick as you go, and an arc at the edge shows where you are.

## Lighter on the battery

- The watch face is black by default and shows only a thin time on the always-on screen. All Hearth complications on the face share one update (one message to your phone), every five minutes or when something changes.
