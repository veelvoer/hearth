- New: Material 3, Google's design language, as a choice on the phone and the computer. Settings → Appearance → Style → Material 3. You get tonal cards, round buttons, filter chips, and the standard navigation bar on the phone or a navigation rail on the computer. The default look stays as it is.
- With Material 3 on the phone, "System colors" takes the colors of your wallpaper. On the computer the accent color you pick becomes the color scheme.
