## Fixed

- **A chat's settings are the same on every device.** The permissions (Auto, Edits, Plan only, Ask me, Full auto), the model and the effort you pick for a chat are now kept on your Hearth server. Pick them on your computer, phone or watch and every other device uses them too. When you say "continue" on your watch, the chat runs exactly as you set it up, Full auto included.
- Chats from before this update take the settings your phone or computer remembered the first time you open them there.
- A voice call can still use its own quick model without changing the chat's model.
