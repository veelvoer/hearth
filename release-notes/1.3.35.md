## Usage limit

- When a chat stops because your usage ran out, you get a notification (phone and desktop) and a card in the chat: continue when your usage is back, or not now.
- New setting "When your usage runs out → Continue by itself": the chat carries on when your usage resets, with a heads-up.

## Root rights

- If the server runs as a service that may not gain rights (NoNewPrivileges), Settings now says so and shows the one command that fixes it. Before, "Check again" kept failing without a clear reason.

## Weekly plan

- Days that are over can no longer be given usage.
- All seven days always show on the slider, also the ones with a small share.

## Fixes

- Highlighted buttons no longer paint over other buttons while you scroll.
