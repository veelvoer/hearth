## Safer runs, and much easier to start

**Safety**
- A copy of the project is kept before every run. In a chat, the undo button puts everything back the way it was: changed files come back, new files go away, and a copy of how it was is kept so you can undo the undo.
- A spending limit per day and per chat: a run that would go past it is refused with a clear message.
- Nightly backups of your chats (switch on in Settings > Server > Safety), with a restore button. A chat that is newer than the backup is never overwritten.
- "Never allow": words such as `rm -rf` that are refused at once without asking, and a list of what was allowed and refused lately.
- The health check also warns when a key or password was left in a project file.

**Starting a chat**
- New chats offer ready questions (write an email, plan a trip, fix my computer, explain an error) and the templates you saved.
- On Monday and Tuesday Home shows how your last 7 days went.

**Phone**
- Share text or a picture from any app to Hearth and it opens a new chat with it.
- A camera button next to Attach, a microphone to dictate a message, and a Read button under a reply to hear it.
- Long-press the app icon for "New chat".
- Settings > Look and feel > "Simple view in chats" hides the tool and command details.

**Computer**
- The same safety settings, rules and weekly recap, a Read button on replies, and the Simple view switch.
