## Shared chats

Work in one chat with a friend, each on your own Claude account.

- **Share a chat**: host it on your server, pick the folder your Claude works in, press Invite and send the link (works once, for 24 hours).
- **Own accounts**: "Send with my account" runs your prompt on your own Claude login and posts the answer into the shared chat. Everyone sees the whole conversation, and each prompt carries what the others said since your last turn.
- **Use the host's account**: a friend taps "Ask the host's Claude". The host sees the prompt, can edit it, chooses how much Claude may do (plan only, edits, ask at every step, Full auto) and presses Run it, or denies it.
- **Full permission**: the host can let a person's prompts run without asking, and switch that off again any time. Remove a friend and they are locked out at once.
- On desktop it is the new **Shared** tab, on the phone **Chats → Shared chats ›**.

A friend's key opens only that one shared chat. The friend's app must be able to reach your server (an HTTPS address works from anywhere). Letting someone run prompts on your account may go against the terms of your Claude plan, so check them first.
