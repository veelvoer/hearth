## What is new

- Watch: a Settings page. Pick the theme: "My watch" (Hearth's own look), "My computer" (the colors of your computer's theme, for example Oxide; the phone or the watch asks your computer) or a preset (Hearth clay, Ocean blue, Forest green, Grape, Mono).
- Watch: update from the watch. Settings → Check for updates → Update. Android asks once to allow installing.
- The watch app works on 32-bit and 64-bit watches (its file contains both).
