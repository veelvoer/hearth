## Everything transforms instead of popping

- Phone: the bottom bar has one pill that slides to the page you pick, the icon pops and the name fades in. Pages slide in the direction you move.
- Phone: icons change into each other (send and stop, the options button), folding cards open smoothly with a turning arrow, cards grow and shrink with their content, and numbers count up to their new value.
- Phone: loading chats shows breathing placeholders in the shape of the list, and new messages slide into place.
- Computer: the chat list glides when chats change order or appear, Settings pages morph into each other, icons answer a click, folding sections open smoothly, and bars and switches ease to their new state.
