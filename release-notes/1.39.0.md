## What is new

- Watch: it now talks to your servers through your phone over Bluetooth instead of its own Wi-Fi, which uses far less battery. When the phone is out of reach it still works on its own connection. Update the phone app too.
- Material 3 on the computer: the places now sit in a floating dock at the bottom with the new-chat button beside it, like on the phone. Big headlines, rounder cards, the usage rings on soft scalloped shapes, and a shape-changing indicator while Claude works.
- Material 3 on phone and computer: "Frosted glass" now turns the dock into frosted glass, the page showing through it blurred (phone: Android 12 and newer).

## Fixed

- Dragging the highlight in the Material 3 navigation on the computer now really switches to that place.
- Some icons (download, close and a few more) were drawn with their lines in the wrong place on the phone.
- Phone: while Claude works you can write and queue your next message without stopping it first. Stop sits beside the send button, and "Send now" on a queued message sends it right away.
- The fix for programs started from the Linux AppImage (1.37.1) is back in.
