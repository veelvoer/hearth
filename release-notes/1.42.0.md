## New

- **Hearth watch face** for round watches: the time, the date, and a ring along the edge that shows your 5-hour Claude usage. Install it from the watch app (Settings, "Install watch face") and pick it on the watch. The usage is also available as a complication on any watch face.
- The watch app is rebuilt for the round screen: every screen sits inside the circle, nothing overlaps or is cut off, and it is much smoother and lighter.
- Approve from the wrist: when Claude asks for permission or has a question, the home screen shows Allow / Deny or the answer choices.
- Reply by voice, quick replies ("Yes, go ahead", "Continue", "Explain shorter") and Stop for a running chat, on the watch.
