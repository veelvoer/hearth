## New

- **Use your own Anthropic API key.** Settings → Safety → "Run Claude on my API key". Claude then runs on your key (paid per use) instead of your Claude account. Only the last 4 characters of the key are ever shown again, and you can switch back to your account at any time.
- **A plan with every task.** Before Claude starts on a task (not when you just ask a question), it makes a short checklist and ticks the steps off as it goes. Open the chat on your watch to follow it: a check for each step that is done, a spinning ring for the step it is working on now, an empty ring for what is still to come. Switch it off in Settings → Safety; when it is off, start a message with /plan to get a plan anyway.
- **One period for all of Home.** At the top of Home (computer and phone) pick Today, 7, 14, 30, 90 days or All time, and every card on the page follows: the numbers, the chart, the projects, models and tools, the cost and the fun facts.

## Fixed

- Notifications from Claude were silent. Messages from Claude (done, a question, it needs your OK) now ring and pop up again. The status bar setting now only hides the live usage.
- Pinned chats did not always show on the watch: pins are kept on one server, and chats from your other servers now get their pins too.
- The frosted glass dock on the phone flickered at every tab change. It now fades smoothly instead of switching between glass and a solid panel.
- The computer app could flash white now and then on Linux. Hearth now turns off the WebKit graphics mode that causes it.
- The fun facts on Home did not load on the computer until you had opened Chats first.
