## Fewer words, better icons on the phone

- The Settings icon is now a proper gear, and History is a clock with an arrow going back.
- Icons stand on their own: buttons like Cancel, Close, Save, Copy, Share, Attach, Remove, Add, Tour, Setup, What changed, Look again and Check for updates show only the icon. Screen readers still say what they do.
- The bottom bar shows the name only for the tab you are on, and the chat filters show their name only when selected.
- Searching with no match now says "No chat matches".
