## New

- **The watch face follows your Hearth theme.** The ring (and the rings of the Hearth complications) take the colour of the theme you pick in the watch app: your watch, your computer, or a preset. Change the theme and the face changes with it.
- **More to set on the watch face.** Long-press the face and choose Edit: the ring source (the 5-hour usage by default, or anything else), two extra spots under the time (the week's usage and the chats that are working or waiting, by default), the time colour and style (regular, thin, bold), the accent colour, and the date on or off.
- Three Hearth complications for any watch face: Claude usage, Claude week, Claude chats.

## Faster

- The watch app asks the phone once for everything the home screen needs (usage, chats, what waits for you) and the phone fetches it side by side, instead of one call after the other. The last numbers and chats show at once when you open the app, and the fresh ones replace them. Opening a chat shows what it showed last time right away.

## Smoother

- Calmer, springier motion like the other Hearth apps: screens slide and scale in, lists and numbers grow in one after the other, the ring and the percentage count up, and buttons squeeze with a small bounce when you press them.
