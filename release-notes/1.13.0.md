## A calmer, more professional look

- New bottom bar: full width, always showing all four places with their names, and the current one marked with a soft pill behind its icon. The same on every phone.
- System colors now look finished: your phone's colors are used for the page, cards, buttons and borders in a consistent way. Before, parts of the app kept Hearth's own beige, which clashed.
- Solid panels are now the default, which looks cleaner and is lighter on the phone. Frosted glass is still there in Settings > Appearance if you like it.
- Folding cards in Settings show their explanation as text again instead of a lone (i) button.
