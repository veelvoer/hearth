## Pin and rename chats, and a feel that answers you back

- Pin a chat to keep it on top of the list. On the phone, press and hold a chat; on the computer, right-click it. A small pin shows next to pinned chats.
- Rename any chat, also the ones friends shared with you. The new name is kept on that device, and an empty name brings the original back.
- The phone gives a light tap on buttons, chips and cards, and a firmer one on press and hold, so it feels like it heard you.
- Computer: deleting a project or all chats of a project asks for the name in a proper dialog. Before, that question could not be shown at all.
