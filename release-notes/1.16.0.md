## Calmer chats on the computer, and a checklist for your reports

- Computer: the chat list is now organised like the apps you know. One filter at the top ("All chats" or one project), and the chats below it grouped by Pinned, Today, Yesterday, the last 7 days, the last 30 days and then by month. Chats that are working come first.
- Computer: the side panel of a chat is closed until you open it, so the chat itself gets the space.
- Reports (owner): your support requests are now a checklist, like Google Tasks but in the app. Open reports are on top with a checkbox; tick one when it is done and it moves to "Done". "Remove done" clears them. Reports that are fixed on the support desk are ticked for you, and a new message from the person opens a ticked one again.
- Your own requests stay in their own part below the checklist.
