## Easier to read and use

- Text is easier to read: the light theme's quiet text and orange buttons are darker (they now meet the 4.5:1 contrast that accessibility guidelines ask for), and on the phone the text is a little larger (16 for normal text, 14 for small). Borders of text fields and chips are clearer.
- Phone: buttons, chips and switches are at least 48dp tall, so they are easier to hit. Screen readers (TalkBack) now announce chips as buttons with their selected state, read each switch together with its label, and describe the icon buttons.
- Desktop: the top bar no longer runs into the status and "New chat" button in narrow windows, and fits down to 820px wide. Small text is a bit larger, controls are at least 32px tall, and notifications in the corner are read by screen readers and can be used with the keyboard.
- Design rules and a checklist for new screens are written down in docs/DESIGN.md, and a test now keeps the colors readable.
