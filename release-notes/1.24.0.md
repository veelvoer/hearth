## Routines: Claude works for you while you are away

- Settings > Routines: give Claude a goal and a schedule (every day at a time, or every few hours) and it works on it by itself, then writes down what it found. A new routine only reads, unless you let it edit files.
- Ready to add: Daily digest, Server check, Weekly security look. Or write your own.
- "What it did" is a timeline of the runs: tap one to open the chat. A routine never starts twice at once, and the spending limits and undo still apply.
- New chats can save the message you typed as a template ("Save this as a template"), and the project of a chat shows its chats, cost and changed files at a glance.
