## What is new

- Material 3 now moves like Material 3 Expressive, on the phone and on the computer. Buttons, cards and rows lean toward your finger or pointer, a soft light follows it, and everything springs back with a little bounce.
- The navigation marker glides to the place you pick, stretches as it moves, and can be dragged along the places. Each icon has its own small move when you tap it.
- Screens and cards arrive on springs, one after another. Switch it off with Motion in Settings.
- Phone: the bottom bar floats, and the add button grows out of it on Chats. Swipe a chat right to pin it or left to delete it (it still asks first). Buttons square up while pressed and chosen chips round into pills.
