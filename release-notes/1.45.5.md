## Fixed

- **Tasks handed to your laptop no longer wait for nothing.** When Claude on your server hands a task to your laptop (installs, adb, anything with your hardware) and the project is not on the laptop yet, the laptop used to wait four minutes and then give up without a word. It now waits at most 45 seconds and then does the task anyway, in a work folder on the laptop, and says so.
- Claude on the server no longer asks you to open a session on your laptop and run commands yourself: it hands the task over by itself.
