## What is new

- **Ask anyone's Claude, in both directions.** In a shared chat the host can now ask a friend to run a prompt on the friend's own account, and friends can ask each other. The person asked sees the prompt, can change it, picks how much Claude may do and presses Run it, or denies it. It runs on their own computer or server, and the answer appears in the chat. They can also choose "Always run this person's prompts without asking".
- **Fixed: the desktop shared chat refreshed all the time**, so you could not type a name, a path or a prompt. It now updates piece by piece: what you type, the prompt you are editing and your scroll position stay exactly where they are, and the join and share forms are never redrawn.
