## What is new

- **Choose whose usage you use, in every shared chat.** Press **Options** in the chat header (desktop and phone) and pick: **my own usage**, **the host's usage**, or **another person's usage**. It is remembered per chat and follows you to your other devices.
- **Your own usage** in someone else's chat runs on your own server, with your own Claude, and the answer appears in the shared chat for everyone, marked with whose Claude said it. Set a folder for it in the same window (or just talk).
- **The host can use a friend's usage too.** Pick the friend's name in Options and send: the friend gets a notification (in the app, a small window on the desktop, a pop-up on the phone), can change your prompt and press Run it or Deny, or choose "always run these without asking". Then their own Claude runs it and the answer shows up in the chat. Friends can ask each other the same way.
- **The host's Claude hears what the others did with their own Claude**, so it never works blind.
- **Shared chats are kept apart.** On the desktop they have their own tab in the project row, on the phone their own chip next to All and Working. They are not mixed with your normal chats any more.
