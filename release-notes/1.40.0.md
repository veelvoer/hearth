## What is new

- Material 3 on the computer: on Chats the dock now sits at the foot of the chat list, so the chat keeps the whole height of the window. On the other screens it floats over the page without pushing anything up.
- Material 3 on the phone: the usage ring sits on a soft scalloped shape, and "Claude is working" shows a shape that slowly turns and changes.
- Your desktop's colors on the Oxide window manager: with "My desktop" as the accent color, Hearth uses Oxide's whole theme (background, surfaces, text and accent) and its light or dark mode.

## Faster and smoother

- Chats open much faster: a chat now opens with its last messages, and "Show earlier messages" loads more. The open chat is no longer fetched again every few seconds while nothing happens, so huge chats no longer time out.
- Smoother on older laptops: hovering no longer runs any animation code, presses measure once, and page entrances run on the graphics chip.
- A computer whose Hearth runs as a background service now gets the fast relay with its next update.

## Fixed

- The "A new version is ready" message kept coming back on a computer that was already up to date.
- The update message no longer covers the message box (Material 3: it now appears at the top right).
- A pinned chat's name no longer touches the pin, and the "All chats" arrow has room.
