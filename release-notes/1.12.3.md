## Icons lined up

- Icons now sit on the edge they belong to: info and arrow buttons hug the right edge of their card, actions under text line up with the text, and the back arrow lines up with the screen edge.
- Chat header, composer and top buttons have the same sizes and spacing. The send and stop buttons use drawn icons instead of characters.
- Settings: Widgets looks like the other cards, the (i) of a folding card sits inside it, and back links use a drawn arrow.
- The chat list: a pin now sits at the end of the row so every title starts at the same place.
- Welcome screens use even margins and tidy bullets.
