## Search inside chats, shared pins, and quicker everything

- Search now also looks inside what was written in your chats. Type two letters or more in the search box and matches show up under "In messages", with the piece of text around them. Works on the phone and the computer.
- Pins and names of chats are kept on your server or computer, so the phone and the computer show the same. What you set before is carried over the first time.
- When you rename a chat on the phone, the old name is selected and the keyboard is up, so you just type.
- Phone: pull down on the chat list to refresh it. A chat you opened a moment ago opens at once and catches up in the background.
- Phone: under the newest reply there are Copy and Share buttons.
- Computer: hover over a reply to copy it. Ctrl or ⌘ + K jumps to the search box, and Ctrl or ⌘ + 1 to 4 switch between Chats, Home, History and Settings.
