## New

- **Improve my prompts.** A new setting (Settings → Safety → "Improve my prompts") with three choices:
  - **Button** (the default): a sparkle button in the message box, on the computer and on the phone. Tap it and Haiku rewrites what you typed with more structure: the goal first, the requirements as short bullets, nothing invented. You read the result, change it if you like, and send it yourself. Tap the button again to undo it.
  - **Always**: every prompt is improved as you send it, on every device (not slash commands, voice turns or very short messages).
  - **Off**: no button, nothing is rewritten.
- It needs the Hearth server on this version.
