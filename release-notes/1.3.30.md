## What is new

- **Shared chats always run on your server.** Hosting a shared chat needs a server, and when you use your own Claude in one (or someone asks for it) it runs on your server too, never on a laptop. So it keeps going when your computer or phone is off.
- **The same shared chats on every device.** The chats you joined, your folder for each, your own Claude chat in each and your "always run" choices are kept on your server, so all your phones and computers show the same list and pick up where the other left off.
- Shared chats without a server show a clear note about what to do.
