# Hearth privacy policy

Hearth is a personal app that runs on your own computer and server. There is no Hearth cloud.

**What stays with you.** Your chats, projects, usage numbers and settings live on your computers, your server and your phone. Hearth never sees your Claude login: usage limits come from what Claude Code reports on your own machine.

**Support.** If you write to support from the app, your email address and message are sent by email to the support mailbox and stored on the support server so we can answer you. We do not sell or share them.

**Google Tasks (optional).** If the owner of a Hearth server connects Google Tasks, Hearth uses Google's Tasks API only to create tasks (title and text of a support request) in that owner's own task list. Hearth requests the single scope `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks`, stores the access token only on the owner's own server, shares it with no one, and uses no Google data for anything else, including advertising or AI training. The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
